Or do it yourself

Prefer to build your own?

No problem — a good plant-based plan isn't complicated. It's the same handful of principles behind every plan above. Here's how to put one together yourself.

1

Start with a target

Roughly: fat loss ~1,600–1,800 kcal, maintenance ~2,000–2,300, muscle gain ~2,500–2,800 — nudge it to your size and how active you are. Then aim for protein around 1.6–2.2g per kg of bodyweight. That's the one number most worth hitting.

2

Anchor every meal with a protein

Build the plate around tofu, tempeh, seitan, edamame, soya mince, lentils, chickpeas, beans or TVP — or a scoop of vegan protein. Get this right and the rest falls into place.

3

Add smart carbs & plenty of veg

Oats, rice, potatoes, wholegrain pasta or quinoa for energy; two big handfuls of veg for fibre, vitamins and staying full.

4

Don't fear healthy fats

A thumb of nuts, seeds, avocado, tahini or olive oil per meal keeps you satisfied and helps you absorb the good stuff.

5

Prep once, eat all week

Batch-cook two or three proteins and a couple of carbs in one go, then mix and match through the week. That's the whole secret to staying consistent.

6

Cover the vegan gaps

Take a B12 supplement (non-negotiable on a plant-based diet), and look into vitamin D, an algae-based omega-3 and iodine. Five minutes of admin that makes the whole thing bulletproof.
